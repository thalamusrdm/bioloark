# Bioloark brand DNA

## Positioning

Bioloark is not a generic plant shop. It is a botanical design studio with a shoppable collection: miniature living worlds, rare plants, tools, and commissioned plant systems for homes, offices, and hospitality spaces.

The useful tension is **gallery calm + nursery confidence**. The gallery creates desire; the nursery supplies clarity, care guidance, availability, and reassurance.

## Visual system

- Core colors already used in the site: ink `#15241e`, forest `#102c22`, moss `#65755d`, cream `#f3f0e8`, paper `#faf8f2`, brass `#c4a66d`.
- Use deep forest for immersive chapters and warm paper for commerce-heavy chapters. Brass is a signal, not a fill color.
- Use an elegant serif for large emotional statements and Assistant for Hebrew navigation, body, controls, and dense product information.
- Photography should feel humid, tactile, close, and authored: moss texture, glass reflections, hands at work, roots, tools, scale, and installations in real interiors.
- Prefer editorial asymmetry, full-bleed chapters, quiet rules, large type, and deliberate negative space. Avoid decorative leaf patterns and generic green gradients.
- Motion should suggest slow growth or reveal: gentle parallax, image scale, staggered entry. It must settle quickly and respect reduced motion.

## Voice

Write Hebrew that is precise, sensory, and confident. Short headlines may be poetic; body copy must become practical quickly.

Preferred:

- “עולם חי, מורכב ביד.”
- “בחרו לפי האור, המקום ורמת התחזוקה.”
- “נלווה אתכם מהבחירה ועד שהמערכת מתייצבת בחלל.”

Avoid vague luxury filler, excessive English labels, horticultural jargon without explanation, and unsupported superlatives. Use “פרימיום” sparingly; demonstrate it through materials, craft, service, and proof.

## Reference synthesis, reviewed 2026-08-23

### Shroomroom — `https://shroomroom.pl/o-nas/`

Useful principles: dark scientific-botanical atmosphere; large alternating chapters; macro organic imagery; clear domain pillars; a feeling of specialists combining nature, laboratory knowledge, and craft.

Translate for Bioloark as: use immersive chapters to explain plants, terrariums, materials, and commissioned systems. Keep the scientific credibility, but make navigation calmer and the content less dense.

Do not copy: its near-black UI, mushroom iconography, exact alternating layout, Polish copy, or source imagery.

### Rootine — `https://rootine.co.il/`

Useful principles: immediate commercial promise; navigation by customer need; condition filters such as light level and pet safety; prominent promotions; WhatsApp support; product breadth made approachable.

Translate for Bioloark as: introduce guided discovery by light, room, experience, care level, and object type. Keep promotional urgency subordinate to the atelier tone.

Do not copy: countdown-led yellow promotion bars, dense mega-navigation, exact benefit rows, or generic nursery marketplace density.

### AirPlants — `https://www.airplants.co.il/`

Useful principles: simple category education; concrete proof numbers; bestselling products with ratings; local-growing and warranty reassurance; care support; gift and business paths; a lightweight “green advisor” quiz.

Translate for Bioloark as: offer a “find my living world” guide, care confidence, delivery expectations, verified proof, and distinct paths for self-purchase, gifts, and commissioned spaces.

Do not copy: its logo, beige/olive palette, hero wording, category names, proof metrics, quiz language, popups, or photography.

## Bioloark differentiators to protect

- Japanese-inspired restraint rather than cheerful mass-market greenery.
- Living composition and spatial design, not only individual plant retail.
- A studio in central Tel Aviv and the possibility of personal consultation.
- Craft, curation, rare materials, and systems thinking.
- An experience that can serve both a first-time terrarium buyer and an architect or hotel decision-maker.
